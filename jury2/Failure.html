Recall that Standard Essential Patent (SEP) gives its holder market power -- most manufacturers will adopt the standard and pay royalties to the SEP holder. However, SEP holders are committed to set a "fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory" (FRAND) royalty rate. PhoneMaker sued NextGen claiming its proposed $15-per-device royalty rate is not FRAND in the United States. NextGen countersued by claiming that PhoneMaker has infringed its SEP and requested compensation.
