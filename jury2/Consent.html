INFORMED CONSENT FORM





RESEARCH PROCEDURES
This research is being conducted to study jury decision making. If you agree to participate in our study, you will be asked to answer a series of survey questions and demographic questions (requiring no more than 30 minutes).



HOW MANY PEOPLE WILL BE STUDIED
Approximately 300 participants.



RISKS
There are no foreseeable risks for participating in this research.



BENEFITS
There are no benefits to you as a participant other than to further research in understanding the jury decision making process.



CONFIDENTIALITY
The data in this study will be confidential. Your name will not be collected. You will be assigned a semi-random alphanumeric string as your ID. All collected data is associated with only this ID. The anonymous data collected could be used for future research without additional consent from participants.

The Institutional Review Board (IRB) committee that monitors research on human subjects may inspect study records during internal auditing procedures and are required to keep all information confidential.
While it is understood that no computer transmission can be perfectly secure, reasonable efforts will be made to protect the confidentiality of your transmission.

The de-identified data could be used for future research without additional consent from participants.
Participants may review Prolifics' website for information about their privacy statement. https://prolific.notion.site/Privacy-and-Legal-at-Prolific-395a0b3414cd4d84a2557566256e3d58



PARTICIPATION
Participants are selected from Prolific. They must be (1) 18-years-old and above, (2) located in the United States, (3) using English as a primary language (4) with access to a computing device connected to the internet.

Your participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw from the study at any time and for any reason. If you decide not to participate or if you withdraw from the study, there is no penalty. There are no costs to you or any other party.

You will be compensated for your time in this study ($5 for completing the study). You may be selected to receive an additional bonus of $5 depending partly on how you and other participants answer some of the questions. The odds of winning the drawing is 10% (so 30 of the 300 participants will earn an additional $5).

The study reference number is STUDY00000481 (GMU)/ AS-IRB-HS 02-25021 (N) (Academia Sinica).
Under the U.S. federal tax law you may have individual responsibilities for disclosing the dollar value of the incentive received on this study.

This research has been reviewed according to George Mason University procedures governing your participation in this research.
